JUNKEKMINAV

This is not your generic indie masterpiece. The goal is not to make another FNAF or generic RPG that ends up being worshipped by normies.

< Back to ESMAIWOL NET.ZONE

> SYNOPSIS

JUNKEKMINAV began as a Ren’Py project and became the first visual novel I have made. It is currently a beta with one chapter available. The game’s concept, aesthetic, and intent remain uncertain, since I programmed and developed it while under the influence of drugs. The game sees itself as a kusoge.

> PROJECT DETAILS

  • Type: Visual novel
  • Title: JUNKEKMINAV
  • Status: In development / to be confirmed
  • Author: ESMAIWOL

> CHARACTERS

Without going too deeply into the details, the cast consists of recolors or modifications of Lucky Star characters. In future updates, I do intend to change their designs enough to make them distinct rather than mere recolors. The first chapter features only three characters: Marutsa, Elys kek, and Pirushka.
